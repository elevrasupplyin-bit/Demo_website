TRADE COMPLIANCE & PAPERWORK

Prepared for International Trade

International agricultural trade depends on accurate, timely documentation aligned with customs and importing authority rules. Depending on the shipment terms and destination country, coordination may encompass the following documents:

Regulatory Note: Actual document requirements vary by product, destination country, transaction structure, and applicable import/export regulations. Specific documentation lists are confirmed prior to order finalization.

DOC / 01

Commercial Invoice

Details transaction value, agreed Incoterms, product description, quantity, and payment terms between exporter and buyer.

DOC / 02

Packing List

Itemizes net weight, gross weight, bag count, packaging format, batch markings, and container load configuration.

DOC / 03

Certificate of Origin

Identifies the country of agricultural origin of the goods where required for customs clearance or tariff assessment.

DOC / 04

Bill of Lading or Air Waybill

Issued by the carrier as receipt of cargo and contract of carriage for sea freight or air freight consignments, as applicable.

DOC / 05

Phytosanitary Documentation

Plant health documentation arranged where required by the importing authority for agricultural plant commodities.

DOC / 06

Laboratory & Compliance Reports

Analytical certificates or product compliance reports arranged per mutually agreed testing parameters, where applicable.

PACKAGING FORMATS

Packaging That Fits Your Requirements

Availability, material, bag size, inner liners, labelling, and customization depend on the specific product, supplier capability, order volume, and destination regulations.

Wholesale & Processing

Bulk Packaging

Designed for spice processors, grinders, and wholesale importers. Typically discussed in PP woven bags, jute sacks, or multi-wall paper bags across standard export weights (such as 25 kg or 50 kg, subject to product density and supplier format).

Moisture & Aroma Protection

Food-Grade Packaging

Suited for ground spices, high-volatile seeds, and foodservice supply chains requiring barrier protection. Options under discussion may include food-grade poly-lined bags, corrugated cartons with inner liners, or heat-sealed pouches where supported by the supplier.

Subject to Volume & Supplier

Private-Label Options

For distributors and regional brands seeking custom pack sizes, carton markings, or buyer-supplied artwork. Feasibility depends on minimum order quantities, packaging lead times, and specific supplier packing lines.
